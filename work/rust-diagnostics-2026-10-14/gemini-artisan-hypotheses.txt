### Static Hypotheses

1. **$O(N^2)$ Scans & Per-Entity Allocation in Working Capital**: In the enterprise loop, matching via `initial.entities.iter().find(...)` incurs $O(N^2)$ scanning, while each enterprise allocates and clones a fresh `Vec<Value>` via `quotes.iter().filter(...).cloned().collect()`.
2. **Combinatorial Simulation in `choose_for_enterprise`**: For each enterprise, nested loops traverse `catalog.methods` $\times$ `m.inputs` with a fixed `for _ in 0..64` stepping loop, repeatedly evaluating stock lookups and `BigInt` quote invoices.
3. **Repeated Serde Parsing in Decision Loops**: Hot loops deserialize dynamic JSON structures on every iteration (e.g., `serde_json::from_value(ready.clone())?` in `plan_artisan_choices` and `integer(&terms["cost_minor"])`) rather than querying strongly typed, pre-indexed fields.
