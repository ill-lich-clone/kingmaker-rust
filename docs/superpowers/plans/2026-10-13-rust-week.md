## Global Constraints

- Calendar: 12×30 days, week 7 days, revision increment exactly 1 after successful complete calculation.
- One raw resource per province, unchanged recipes, labor rules, shares, budgets, transport times, prices and economic quantities.
- RAM, source read-only, no fullworld weeks until checkpoint and autonomous runner are verified; goal stays active with target10.
- Preserve authored/unknown fields and typed IDs; no invented initial stores, jobs, state orders or money.
- NotMeasured is explicit; miniature serialization is not a fullworld save.
- SourceArc retained; no full digest, world export or SQL persistence each phase/week.

## Review Focus

1. Empty and partial phases still produce required history; failure in the last phase leaves money, stock, date and revision unchanged.
2. New production and changed methods/capacity enter sales/storage dynamically while protected inputs and manually authored data retain their contracts.
3. Opening budget, investment, artisan capital and payroll use one real cash balance in source order; shared owners cannot gain an extra payment or lose a legitimate expense.
4. Cohort/migration and arrivals before production preserve opening stock/cargo provenance, beneficiaries, consent and whole-week accounting.
5. Replay after save/continuation or boundary tampering must never duplicate consumption, wages, issuance, construction or date; distinct money currencies and calendar rollover remain exact.
